Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 24, 2025

YourSA (“YourSA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to protecting the privacy of our donors, program participants, volunteers, partners, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you interact with our website yoursa.org or participate in our programs.

I. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our website, online forms, donations, event registrations, newsletters, and other interactions with YourSA. It does not apply to third-party websites or services not controlled by us.

II. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Mailing address
  • Donation details (processed by third-party payment providers)
  • Volunteer and program participation information
  • Communications sent to us

Information Collected Automatically

We may collect information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URLs, and timestamps to improve website performance and user experience.

III. How We Use Information

  • Operate and improve our programs and website
  • Process donations and issue acknowledgments
  • Communicate program updates and organizational news
  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

YourSA does not sell, rent, or trade personal information of donors or participants.

IV. Information Sharing

We may share personal information only with trusted service providers, when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of YourSA and others.

V. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission is completely secure.

VI. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at privacy@yoursa.org. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable U.S. and Massachusetts laws.

VII. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law.

VIII. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
